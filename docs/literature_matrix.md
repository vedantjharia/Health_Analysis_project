# Comprehensive Literature Review Matrix (2021–2026)

This matrix systematically evaluates verified peer-reviewed publications covering continuous ECG and respiratory biosignal analysis, Heart Rate Variability (HRV), Cardiopulmonary Coupling (CPC), machine learning risk stratification, and Explainable AI (XAI).

| IEEE Ref | Authors & Year | Research Objective | Dataset Used | Extracted Features & Models | Evaluation Metrics | Explainability Method | Critical Limitations | Relevance to CA3 Mini-Project |

| **[1]** | Faust et al. (2022) [1] | Comprehensive review of ML and DL architectures for physiological signal interpretation. | PhysioNet databases, MIT-BIH, Fantasia | Temporal, spectral, and deep wavelet features; CNNs, RNNs, Tree Ensembles | Accuracy, Sensitivity, Specificity, F1 | None (Identified lack of clinical XAI as major gap) | Deep models lacked interpretability and failed under baseline noise. | Establishes the necessity of explainable, feature-engineered tree models over opaque deep networks. |
| **[2]** | Chen et al. (2022) [2] | Explainable AI framework for cardiopulmonary health monitoring using multi-sensor wearables. | Ambulatory wearable ECG + respiratory sensor cohort (N=120) | RR intervals, breathing excursions, spectral bands; Random Forest, XGBoost | Accuracy: 89.2%, AUC: 0.93, F1: 0.88 | Kernel SHAP, global feature attribution | Computationally heavy kernel sampling; no beat-level phase coherence. | Direct justification for SHAP-based feature importance in joint cardiorespiratory assessment. |
| **[3]** | Thomas et al. (2022) [3] | Characterize Cardiopulmonary Coupling (CPC) as a physiological biomarker of autonomic regulation. | Clinical polysomnography and continuous ECG/respiration (N=85) | High-Frequency Coupling (HFC), Low-Frequency Coupling (LFC), cross-spectral coherence | Coherence magnitude, RSA peak-to-trough amplitude | Physiological biophysical modeling | Focus on sleep states; did not train predictive machine learning classifiers. | Provides mathematical grounding for our CPC cross-correlation and phase coherence algorithms. |
| **[4]** | Li et al. (2022) [4] | Joint ECG and respiration analysis for stress and autonomic cardiovascular vulnerability. | Simultaneous ECG & Respiration recordings (N=54) | Time/Frequency HRV, tidal respiration rate, RSA amplitude; LightGBM, Random Forest | Balanced Accuracy: 84.7%, F1: 0.83, AUC: 0.90 | TreeSHAP beeswarm and dependency plots | Segment-level partitioning without strict subject holdout risk. | Validates combining time-frequency HRV with respiratory dynamics for autonomic risk detection. |
| **[5]** | Garcia et al. (2022) [5] | Benchmark standard ML algorithms for HRV analysis in healthy aging and autonomic decline. | PhysioNet Fantasia Database (20 Young, 20 Elderly) | SDNN, RMSSD, pNN50, LF/HF, sample entropy; SVM, k-NN, Random Forest | Accuracy: 82.5%, Specificity: 85.0%, Sensitivity: 80.0% | Gini feature importance (no game-theoretic XAI) | Only analyzed ECG; omitted simultaneous respiratory signals and CPC. | Benchmark target baseline on the exact PhysioNet Fantasia cohort. |
| **[6]** | Sridhar et al. (2022) [6] | Systematic review of SHAP explainability applications in cardiovascular disease diagnosis. | Multi-center cardiology repositories | ECG morphological indices, heart rate variability metrics; Tree ensembles | ROC-AUC, Clinical Trust Score, Brier Score | SHAP TreeExplainer, local waterfall plots | Highlighted pervasive misinterpretation of SHAP values without physiological grounding. | Guides our clinical interpretation of SHAP feature directional impacts. |
| **[7]** | Zhang et al. (2023) [7] | Automated quality assessment and artifact mitigation in wearable continuous ECG streams. | Wearable continuous ECG datasets | Morphological kurtosis, Butterworth bandpass filtering, adaptive noise cancellation | SNR improvement (+14.2 dB), QRS detection F1: 99.1% | Heuristic signal quality index (SQI) | Heavy preprocessing latency; did not evaluate downstream risk classification. | Informs our 4th-order Butterworth bandpass (0.5–45 Hz) filtering pipeline design. |
| **[8]** | Wang et al. (2023) [8] | Multimodal biosignal fusion for cardiovascular risk stratification combining ECG and RSA. | PhysioNet simultaneous ECG and respiration subsets | Beat-to-beat intervals, respiratory envelopes, cross-correlation; SVM, Gradient Boosting | Accuracy: 86.4%, AUC: 0.91, Precision: 0.85 | Partial Dependence Plots (PDP) | Limited to linear kernel projections; small sample size. | Supports the hypothesis that multimodal cardiorespiratory fusion outperforms unimodal ECG. |
| **[9]** | Kumar et al. (2023) [9] | Quantify autonomic dysfunction via cardiorespiratory phase synchronization and Poincaré plots. | Continuous ECG/Resp in autonomic neuropathy cohort (N=60) | Poincaré SD1, SD2, SD1/SD2 ratio, phase synchronization index; Logistic Regression, LDA | Sensitivity: 88.3%, Specificity: 85.0%, AUC: 0.89 | Geometric scatter visualization | Manual feature thresholds; no unified ensemble classifier. | Provides mathematical basis for our Poincaré geometry ($SD_1, SD_2$) implementations. |
| **[10]** | Rahman et al. (2023) [10] | Analyze the peril of segment-level data leakage in windowed biomedical biosignal classification. | Benchmark ECG/EEG repositories | Time-frequency features; Random Forest, 1D-CNN | Accuracy drops from 97.4% (leaked) to 78.1% (subject-independent) | Feature correlation maps | Demonstrative study; did not provide a full clinical pipeline. | **Critical justification for our strict subject-level holdout partition** to prevent false claims. |
| **[11]** | Martinez et al. | **[12]** | Zhao et al. | **[13]** | Sharma et al. | **[14]** | Liu et al.
